## Guides and Features
- [Cryptocurrency Buying & Selling]: Commission-free trading of BTC, ETH, and USDC via Interac e-Transfer.
- [Shakepay Card](https://shakepay.com/card): Visa prepaid card earning up to 1.5% back in bitcoin with #shakepaid rewards.
- [ShakingSats Rewards]: Daily rewards feature where users shake their phone to earn free bitcoin.
- [Recurring Buys]: Automated dollar-cost averaging on daily, weekly, or monthly schedules with no fee or spread on bitcoin buys
- [Shakepay Interest]: Competitive interest rates paid weekly in bitcoin on CAD and USD balances.
- [Peer to Peer Transfers]: Instant money transfers to anyone on Shakepay or via e-Transfer in Canada.
- [Shakepay Blue](https://shakepay.com/blue): Shakepay’s best benefits to grow your bitcoin balance faster. More card cash back, and no FX fee. Higher interest on cash, better ShakingSats rewards, and priority support.
- [Shakepay for business](https://shakepay.com/business): Integrate crypto into your business seamlessly. Security-first approach, dedicated business support, fast money movement, simple and transparent platform
- [Shakepay Private](https://shakepay.com/private): our best volume-based and FX pricing, instant access to funds, deep liquidity, white glove support.

* Customer are Canadian or based in Canada (requirement)
